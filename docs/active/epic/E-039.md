## Intent — the bigger-picture play

E-038 produced a repetition inventory, and several hot spots (C-05 through C-08) now demonstrably obscure where signals enter the scheduler, how the scheduler moves through failure / reclaim / timeout / liveness, and where artifacts, ticket completion, and provenance get published. The play is a focused, evidence-first code-cleanliness and architectural-refactoring field pass executed on the next Lisa release candidate's Codex seats: introduce a typed signal-ingestion boundary that keeps payload, deletion, and lease differences distinct; name the scheduler's failure and recovery transitions without weakening scheduler authority; and make artifact / ticket-completion / provenance publication an explicit atomic boundary. Every structural edit is bracketed — characterization tests that fix current behavior BEFORE, regression tests AFTER — so observable behavior, public CLI contracts, installer ownership, and provider-specific semantics stay exactly as they are. It matters now because these three boundaries are precisely where real field failures (misassignment, false delivery errors, done-not-committed residue, provenance drift) originate: clarifying them converts latent, repetition-hidden risk into legible, testable contracts on the RC before it ships.

## Value to the design

Realizes lower-risk evolvability of the three boundaries most implicated in observed field failures, plus the retirement of a known hygiene debt (13 pre-existing test-only all-target/all-feature Clippy findings). Advances P2 (ingestion, lifecycle, and publication states correspond to durable reality; failure stays visible and recoverable), P3 (assignment/liveness/completion/recovery parity stays equivalent from the scheduler and operator view across Claude and Codex), P4 (named transitions and an explicit publication boundary make ownership, progress, and failure easier to supervise at a glance), and P5 (characterization plus regression fixtures turn prior field evidence into durable, deterministic regression proof).

## Done looks like

The RC's Codex seats have executed the pass end to end; native and WASM fmt / clippy / test gates are green, including the 13 previously-known test-only Clippy findings now cleared as a bounded hygiene item; the CLI plus embedded WASM are rebuilt; observable behavior, public CLI contracts, and provider-specific semantics are unchanged, proven by characterization tests that pass identically before and after each structural change; and a field report exists that cleanly separates deterministic proof from observations of live Codex-seat execution — accounting for assignment/reuse failures, retries, timeouts, stale panes, false delivery errors, staged/index residue, done-not-committed residue, and provenance integrity. Any behavior change, unexplained field anomaly, or failed acceptance is recorded explicitly and blocks a Done.
