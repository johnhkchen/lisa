## Context

The desk earns its name only if reaching and acting on it is reflex. Key estate today: [p] cycles presets (crates/lisa-plugin/src/lib.rs ~8187, `ViewPreset::next` ui.rs ~365), [d]/[r] open modals, [space] pauses, j/k scroll, Shift+D dumps state, q quits — plenty of letters free per the chrome inventory. Rewire: [p] jumps straight to the Present desk from any view — one keypress, always; preset cycling moves to [v] (or research's better candidate among the free letters). On the desk: Up/Down move the card cursor, Enter expands/collapses, [d] opens T-053-01-02's reason flow scoped to the selected card's ticket, [s] sends a blocked ticket back for another review pass — the in-plugin equivalent of `lisa unblock`'s flip (crates/lisa-cli/src/unblock.rs ~41: Blocked → open for re-review) — after which the card leaves the desk on the next poll. Selection and scroll reset sanely on view switch (the existing `scroll_offset` reset pattern, ~8189). The status line tells the truth about all of it (ui.rs ~1216): every hint names a key that does what it says.

_Advances: sign or send back, two keys, from anywhere — the decision loop closes at reflex speed (P4)._
