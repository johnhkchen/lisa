## Context

On 2026-07-16 two review-blocked tickets whose remedies were external (an unpublished release; human-run tests) were re-seated by the scheduler indefinitely, occupied both `max_threads=2` seats, and starved every ready ticket until an operator hand-edited `status: blocked` into their frontmatter. Formalize that hand edit as scheduler policy (crates/lisa-plugin/src/scheduler.rs, using the existing `status: blocked` scheduling exclusion at crates/lisa-core/src/dag.rs:197): agent-owned blocks get a small fixed number of re-attempts per loop, then park; operator- and world-owned blocks park immediately with the structured block carried alongside; world-owned parks are marked recheck-eligible (execution of checks belongs to T-048-02-02). Parked tickets never take a seat and are never silently dropped — they remain visible board state. Park and unpark append the T-048-01-01 provenance records.

_Advances: recovery becomes bounded across attempts, not just within one — churn stops masquerading as progress and seats go to workable tickets; parking is scheduling visibility, never a substitute for real completion transitions._

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A replay test of the 2026-07-16 shape (two seats; one operator-owned and one world-owned block; ready tickets queued) asserts: both blocks park, neither is ever re-seated, both seats go to the ready tickets, and park provenance rows appear.
- [ ] The agent-owned case retries exactly its configured bound then parks; the bound and each retry are visible in provenance; unparking (status back to open) makes the ticket schedulable again without any special-case state.
