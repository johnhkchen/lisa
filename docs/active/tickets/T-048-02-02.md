## Context

Two halves of keeping humans out of the loop when they're not truly needed. (1) Ask authoring: the Review-phase instructions injected into agent sessions (crates/lisa-cli/src/templates.rs) teach block-writing agents the structured fields and the language rule — remedy owner honestly assigned; the ask one sentence addressed to a person who didn't do the work, naming the action rather than the subsystem; a `check` supplied whenever the remedy is externally observable. Quote the 2026-07-16 block reasons as counter-examples ("no stable Pages artifact has been deployed" → "Lisa needs the release published; run: just release. Lisa will notice on its own once it's live."). (2) Auto-recheck: at loop start and on the existing timer cadence, world-owned parked tickets run their checks (read-only, timeout-bounded); a pass self-clears the park with an unpark provenance row — zero operator commands. Depends on T-047-01-01 to serialize templates.rs edits.

_Advances: when no human judgment is truly needed, no human is asked — world-owned blocks clear themselves; when a human is needed, the agent that blocked is the one who must say so in plain words._
