## Context

**Read `S-074-01` first.**

`crates/lisa-cli/src/init.rs:1618`:

```rust
let ready = dag.get_ready_tickets();
ready_count = ready.len();

// 12. At least one ready ticket
if ready.is_empty() {
    diagnostics.push(ValidationDiagnostic {
        path: format!("{}/", ticket_dir_rel),
        category: "readiness",
        message: "no tickets with phase 'ready' and all dependencies satisfied"
            .to_string(),
        severity: Severity::Error,
    });
}
```

The ready set being empty is the *symptom*. The two causes underneath it are
opposites, and everything needed to tell them apart is already loaded at this
point in the function — `scan.tickets` is in hand and the DAG is built.

## The two cases

- **Finished** — no ready tickets and none open. The board did its job. Not a
  diagnostic at all, or at most an informational line. Exit zero.
- **Stalled** — no ready tickets and open ones remain. Every one of them is
  blocked or waiting on an unsatisfied dependency. This is a real error, keeps
  its severity, and is worth saying *more* about than it does today: which
  tickets are stuck and what they are waiting on is the operator's next question
  and lisa is holding the answer.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **A board with every ticket done exits zero and says so in words a person can
  act on** — nothing waiting, count done — rather than `1 error(s) found. Fix and
  re-run`.
- **A board with open tickets and nothing schedulable still fails**, and names at
  least one stuck ticket and what it waits on. This case getting quieter would be
  a worse bug than the one being fixed.
- **The word `error` is not used for the finished case anywhere in the output**,
  including the trailing summary line and the process's own `Error:` line, which
  is what the operator actually reads first.
- **Nothing else about `validate` changes.** Cycles, missing dependencies and
  every other diagnostic keep their severity and wording.
- **`lisa status` and `validate` agree.** On 2026-08-16 `status` said *"Nothing
  waiting"* on the same board `validate` called an error. Two commands giving
  opposite readings of one board is the thing being fixed, not a detail.
- Reproduce it: run `validate` on a board with everything done — exit 0, no error
  wording. Then reopen one ticket with an unsatisfiable dependency and have it
  fail, naming that ticket.

## Notes

- **Measured 2026-08-16**: four boards on one desk (`lisa` 235/235, `steer`
  22/22, `sf-muni-routes` 15/15, `renderer` 38/38) all reported `1 error(s)
  found` after finishing their overnight work. The morning read of the fleet was
  *four boards are broken*.
- Worth checking while in here whether anything **scripts** `lisa validate` and
  gates on its exit code — a finished board currently fails such a gate, and that
  failure would be silent and occasional rather than obvious.
- The check's original intent is sound and should be kept in the write-up: a
  board nobody can schedule work on is worth shouting about. It just needs to
  know which board it is looking at.
