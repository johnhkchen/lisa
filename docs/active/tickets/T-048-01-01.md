## Context

A review block today is `{disposition: "block", reason: "<engineering prose>"}` (crates/lisa-core/src/disposition.rs) — it answers neither who owns the remedy, nor what the ask is in words a bystander can act on, nor how Lisa would know the remedy happened. Extend the block shape with optional fields: `remedy_owner` ("agent" | "operator" | "world"), `ask` (one plain-language sentence addressed to a person who didn't do the work), `steps` (optional list of concrete actions), `check` (optional read-only shell probe, exit 0 = remedy done; stored, never executed at parse time). Parsing stays fail-closed and gains a churn-safe default: a block with missing or malformed structure parses as operator-owned with the raw `reason` as its ask, flagged `unstructured`. Existing plain blocks must keep parsing byte-compatibly. Provenance gains park/unpark transition records with timestamps so stranded-human time becomes queryable.

_Advances: a block becomes three answerable questions instead of a paragraph — states correspond to durable reality; additive schema, fail-closed, no behavior change in this ticket._

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Unit tests: all three remedy owners parse; steps/check optional; malformed or ownerless blocks → operator-owned + `unstructured` flag; legacy `{disposition, reason}` blocks parse unchanged; `check` content is never executed during parsing.
- [ ] Park/unpark provenance record types exist with ticket, attempt, owner class, and timestamps, unit-tested for append and replay.
