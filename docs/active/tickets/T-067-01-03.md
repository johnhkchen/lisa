## Context

**Read `S-067-01` first.** `T-067-01-01` is why panes die. This is what a loop
should do about it while it is still running.

Measured on `screen-design`, 2026-08-13, after two panes were lost to a refused
probe:

```
lisa-5            → 4 children    healthy
screen-design-4   → 2 children    running on half its panes
```

`.lisa-layout.kdl` declares four panes and creates them **once**, at
`lisa loop`. The plugin has never created one since. So a lost pane is gone for
the session's lifetime, and `pane_count = max_threads * 2` — whose own comment is
*"extra idle panes absorb new tickets while finishing panes wind down"* — silently
becomes `max_threads * 1` with no spare at all.

**The only recovery today is restarting the loop**, which costs every attempt in
flight. On this board that was two agents mid-ticket.

### It is not architecturally out of reach

The plugin already asks zellij for exactly the permissions this needs:

```rust
PermissionType::WriteToStdin,
PermissionType::ChangeApplicationState,
PermissionType::ReadApplicationState,
PermissionType::RunCommands,
```

and already manipulates panes — `rename_terminal_pane`, `rename_plugin_pane`. It
has simply never made one.

The operator's framing:

> *"no reason we can't inform a running lisa loop via `rail` that it might want to
> attempt a regeneration procedure, though? this could be a self healing
> mechanism"*

## Acceptance Criteria

- **A loop notices it has fewer panes than its layout declared.** Say what it
  counts and how often; a pane is lost rarely and the check must not be a poll
  that costs anything.
- **It regenerates the missing pane into the same stack**, at the same size, so
  the arrangement after healing matches the one after launch. A replacement pane
  outside the stack is a new bug wearing the fix's clothes.
- **Nothing in flight is disturbed.** Agents in surviving panes keep their seats,
  their leases and their geometry. This is the whole reason to heal rather than
  restart.
- **Regeneration is bounded and says so.** A pane that dies immediately on being
  recreated must not loop — say how many attempts, over what window, and what the
  loop does when it gives up.
- **A regenerated pane is a fresh seat, not a resumed one.** The attempt that died
  with the old pane is over; `T-067-01-02` requires that its end be recorded, and
  healing must not paper over a lost attempt by quietly reusing its seat.
- **`rail` can ask for it and does not perform it.** rail's own invariants forbid
  creating geometry inside zellij (`no-zellij-split`), and lisa's plugin is the
  only thing that legitimately can. Say what the request looks like and what
  answers it — *asked and healed*, *asked and refused*, *asked and already fine*.
- Reproduce it: kill a pane in a live loop and assert four panes and a working
  seat afterwards, with the surviving attempts untouched.

## Notes

- **This is the cheap half of the fix and `T-067-01-01` is the important one.** A
  loop that heals a pane it should never have lost is better than one that does
  not, and worse than one that does not lose it. Both are worth having; neither
  substitutes for the other.
- The layout is the source of truth for how many panes a run should have, and it
  is written per-run into `.lisa-layout.kdl`. Whatever counts should read that
  rather than recomputing `max_threads * 2` and hoping they agree.
- Worth deciding in review: whether healing should also apply after the operator
  closes a pane by hand. That is indistinguishable from a crash at the zellij
  level, and an operator who deliberately closed a pane will not enjoy it coming
  back.
