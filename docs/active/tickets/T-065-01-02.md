## Context

**Read `S-065-01` first.**

Lisa has a word for every other thing that happens to a ticket — `validate`,
`status`, `unblock`, `already-done`, `reset-ticket`, `commit-ticket`,
`complete-ticket`, `claim`, `proposal` — and no word for **making one**.

Filing today means hand-authoring frontmatter, choosing an id that does not
collide, guessing the story's `tickets:` list needs updating too, and finding out
from `lisa validate` afterwards whether any of it was right. Every ticket in this
repository and in `screen-design` was created that way, including this one.

**That is fine for a person with an editor open and wrong for everything else.**
The desk is building two consumers that need to file:

- `steer cross` already turns a chosen proposal into a draft that passes
  `ask-lint` and `premises`, and has nowhere to hand it
- `screen-design`'s `S-014` wants a slot that files work when the board runs dry

Both would otherwise write markdown by string-formatting and hope, which is how a
frontmatter convention becomes an undocumented API with three implementations.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **A word that writes a ticket and validates before it lands**, not after. A
  ticket that would fail `lisa validate` is refused with the reason, and nothing
  is written.
- **Id allocation is lisa's job, not the caller's.** Two callers filing at once
  must not collide, and a caller must not have to read the directory to guess the
  next number.
- **The story's ticket list stays consistent**, or the tie is explicitly declared
  the caller's problem in writing. Today it is a silent hand-edit that `validate`
  does not catch.
- **The body comes from the caller and lisa does not compose prose.** Filing is
  bookkeeping — frontmatter, id, placement, validation. Whatever writes the
  Context and Acceptance Criteria is upstream and stays upstream.
- **It works from a pipe.** `steer cross` produces a draft on stdout; the natural
  shape is reading one rather than requiring a file the caller has already
  written.
- Filing into a board with a **running loop** is safe and is the expected case —
  see `T-065-01-01`, which is what makes the filed ticket actually run.
- Says what it did in a form a program can read, because the callers are programs.

## Notes

- Not a ticket *generator*. Whether an agent's own idea reaches the board without
  a person seeing it is the fork the operator deferred in `screen-design`'s
  `S-014`, and nothing here decides it. This word files what it is given.
- The frontmatter shape is already a contract with three consumers reading it
  (`lisa`, `steer`, `rail`) and no writer. This gives it one.
- Worth checking whether `proposal` is the seam this belongs beside rather than a
  new word — it already settles first-responder proposals for waiting tickets, and
  two words for *work that does not exist yet* would be one too many.
