## Context

The calm surface for the severity dial. Notes filed by T-049-06-01's disposition class are batched into a **Notes for you** queue, rendered clearly apart from Waiting on you in both `lisa status` (crates/lisa-cli/src/status.rs) and the dashboard (crates/lisa-plugin/src/ui.rs): Waiting on you means the line needs the operator now; Notes for you means the line kept moving and has something to say when the operator chooses to listen. Each entry leads with the plain one-sentence summary (ask-floor rules from T-049-05-02 apply), with the quoted criterion and evidence citation beneath. Notes gate nothing — no scheduling, seat, or completion effect. Settling is explicit and recorded: an acknowledgment command (`lisa notes` to list, an ack to clear) writes a provenance row; a dispute the operator decides is real work graduates by ordinary ticket authoring, not by any automatic mutation. The queue derives from durable journal state, so it survives restarts and is level-triggered like everything else this epic touches — no in-memory-only note may exist.

_Advances: what the line wants the operator to know arrives batched, plain, and on the operator's schedule — the andon board, not the andon cord._

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Rendering tests: notes appear under their own heading in status and dashboard, plain summary first, visibly distinct from Waiting on you; an empty queue renders nothing; entries derive from the journal after a restart (fixture kills and reloads the plugin).
- [ ] Lifecycle tests: `lisa notes` lists entries; acknowledging writes a provenance row and clears the entry durably; acknowledged notes never resurface; notes have zero effect on scheduling, seats, or completion in fixture assertions.
