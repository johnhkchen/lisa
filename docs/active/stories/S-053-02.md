**Scope:** One place where decisions wait, presented like memos. (1) **The desk view.** A new Present preset renders one collapsed card per pending decision — parked blocks (`collect_parked_remedies`, parking.rs ~121), attention-box review waits (Review-phase tickets, ui.rs ~522), unread notes (`collect_notes`, lib.rs ~8804), and the class those sources miss — Blocked tickets with no parseable disposition (invisible to the remedy collector, parking.rs ~118), exactly the ones the no-review override serves. Collapsed card ≤3 lines: ticket id + short title, age (E-052's formatter; "—" where no stamp exists), the one-sentence ask the disposition schema mandates, and the recommended key. Expanding the selected card reveals the staff work — reason, criterion quote, evidence path, steps, check command. The Operations view's three sprawling sections shrink to one-line pointers with counts ("5 waiting, 2 notes — [p]"), so the paragraphs leave the default screen entirely. Empty desk says "Nothing needs you." (2) **The keys.** [p] becomes go-to-desk from anywhere (one keypress); preset cycling moves to a free key ([v] suggested). On the desk: Up/Down select, Enter expand/collapse, [d] opens the reason flow scoped to the selected card's ticket, [s] sends a blocked ticket back for another review pass (the in-plugin equivalent of `lisa unblock`'s flip, unblock.rs ~41), and the card leaves the desk on the next poll.

**Story acceptance:** With two parked blocks, one review wait, one note, and one fail-closed block: the desk shows five collapsed cards, no criterion text visible; Enter reveals it on one card only. From Operations: [p] → [d] → Enter seals the recommended action in four keypresses. [s] on a parked card returns the ticket to review and the card leaves the desk. Operations shows pointer lines, not paragraphs; status-line hints match the new key estate exactly.

**Honest boundary:** Cards render fields the dispositions already carry — no new prose generation, no summarization pass (N4); a jargony ask surfaces verbatim as the disposition author's bug to fix upstream. Notes are receipts, not decisions — they never grow action keys beyond expand/dismiss. No mouse, no multi-select.
