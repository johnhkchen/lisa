**Scope:** A pan for the map that outgrows even condensed labels. Vertical scrolling exists (`scroll_offset` lib.rs ~896, j/k ~8219, skip/take in `print_dashboard` ~1660); horizontal has no counterpart, so overflow is pure loss. Add a DAG-view-only horizontal offset on [h]/[l] (plus Left/Right), clamped to `widest_line − cols`, reset on view switch, inert when the map fits and in every other view. The named hazard: by slice time the lines carry injected ANSI, and a byte cut shears escape sequences into garbage — the slicer counts visible columns (slice raw then re-colorize, or ANSI-aware cutting; design decides and records why). The S-054-01 indicator line gains the key names.

**Story acceptance:** On a condensed-but-still-wide fixture: [l] reveals clipped columns, [h] returns, both clamp at the edges, offset resets on view switch. A fixture walking every pan offset over colored lines asserts intact escape sequences and correct visible text at each cut. Keys do nothing in other views and on a fitting map.

**Honest boundary:** Panning is a viewport, not a layout change (N4) — no re-layout on pan, no follow-the-cursor, no minimap. Bindings mirror the existing vertical pattern; no new config.
