**Scope:** Remove the scheduler's in-place session-reuse machinery now that no shipped adapter can reach it. The 0.4.4-rc.8 flip moved native Claude to `ResetStrategy::ExitThenFresh` — the same per-ticket fresh-process boundary Codex has always used — because in-place `/clear` reuse kept the launch line's `LISA_TICKET_ID`/`LISA_ATTEMPT_ID` environment alive for the pane's whole life, leaving every ticket after the first under a stale identity (agents read the wrong ticket; usage capture mis-attributed). That leaves `TransitionState::WaitingForStop`/`WaitingForClear`, the stop/clear timeout fallbacks in `check_transition_timeouts`, the `ClearHandshake` reuse arm in `schedule_ready_tickets`, and `handle_cleared_signal`'s prompt delivery unreachable in production — machinery that reads as live but never runs (P2's exact complaint). Evidence first: research must cite at least one ExitThenFresh field leg (0.4.4-rc.8+, multiple consecutive Claude tickets on one pane, journal-sealed clean) before deletion; absent that evidence the ticket parks early with a plain note naming the one leg to run. Design decides whether `ResetStrategy::ClearHandshake` survives as a documented seam for future in-place-reset integrations or goes entirely.

**Story acceptance:** No scheduler path can send `/clear` as a scheduling transport; the removed states and fallbacks are gone or provably unreachable-and-documented; every deleted test maps in writing to its successor or to the removed path it covered; `.cleared` emission survives wherever a shipped adapter still consumes it; WASM check, workspace tests, and clippy all green.

**Honest boundary:** This is a removal, not a redesign (N4) — the recycle path's behavior does not change by one byte. Codex-side hooks and signals stay untouched. If the field record shows anything still exercising the machinery, the ticket stops and reports instead of deleting around it.
