**Scope:** The shared attribution contract and the ownership lookup it will be attributed against — no writer or consumer behavior change yet. Two disjoint seams: (a) a new append-only per-capture record type in crates/lisa-core (the successor to the overwrite `usage_artifact`/`<key>.usage.json` shape emitted by capture_usage.rs and read by lib.rs read_usage), carrying only the honest facts a capturing process knows — pane_id, session_id, captured_at, input/output tokens; and (b) a pane×time ownership lookup in crates/lisa-plugin over the scheduler's already-durable per-attempt `(pane_id, started_at, ended_at, ticket_id)` intervals held in the thread table / provenance records.

**Story acceptance:** lisa-core round-trips a CaptureRecord and two captures for the same pane append as two JSONL rows that never overwrite; and the plugin's owner_at resolves a timestamp inside a recycled pane's first-ticket window to ticket A, inside its later second-ticket window to ticket B, and outside any owned window to None — all proven by unit tests with no writer or consumer wired to the new shape yet.

**Honest boundary:** Contract + lookup only: after this story the capture writer still emits the old overwritten shape and the plugin still reads the old `<ticket>.usage.json` path — wiring them is S-043-02 and S-043-03. Everything here is fixture-proven and FREE (unit tests, no tokens, no live loop).
