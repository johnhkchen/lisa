## Context

Lisa needs a landing page aimed at brand-new visitors who are not deeply
technical: people trying to build a project (an app, a tool, a business idea)
who have heard "AI coding agent" but don't know why they'd want one running
through a ticket board instead of just chatting with it directly.

The page must be bottom-line-first: lead with the outcome and the reason to
care, before any protocol or workflow detail. Visitors should not need to
understand phases, DAGs, git sealing, or Zellij to understand why Lisa is
useful to them. Technical depth belongs below the fold or in linked docs, not
in the opening pitch.

## Goals

- A brand-new, non-technical visitor understands within one screen what
  problem Lisa solves for them and why it matters to their project.
- The page builds trust (safety, control, reversibility) without requiring
  the visitor to understand how that trust is implemented.
- Deeper/technical readers who scroll further still find accurate,
  ungimmicked detail — nothing in the simplified copy misrepresents what
  Lisa does.

## Non-Goals

- Not a product/API reference or CLI documentation.
- Not targeted at experienced Lisa users or contributors.
