## Context

Write a short FAQ / trust section answering the objections a non-technical,
brand-new visitor is likely to have before they'll try Lisa: safety of
their code/project, what happens if something goes wrong, whether they need
to understand the underlying tooling first, and cost/effort to get started.
Draw the specific worries from the T-002 brief.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Each objection listed in the T-002 brief's "concerns" has a corresponding
  question-and-answer pair, phrased as the visitor would actually ask it
  (not restated in technical terms).
- Answers are honest and specific enough to be checked against the actual
  project behavior (e.g., what "undoable" or "reviewable" means in
  practice) without requiring the visitor to read source docs first.
- No answer overstates what Lisa does or promises a guarantee the project
  doesn't make; if precision requires a caveat, the caveat is stated in
  plain language, not omitted.
- Copy is reviewed against T-003's outline to confirm it does not overlap
  with T-005 (benefits) or T-006 (how it works).
