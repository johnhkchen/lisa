## Context

Assemble the approved copy from T-004 (hero/CTA), T-005 (benefits), T-006
(how it works), and T-007 (FAQ/trust) into a single static landing page,
laid out per T-003's outline order. This ticket owns visual design and
markup; it does not rewrite the approved copy's meaning, only formats and
presents it.

This is the page a small product would proudly ship, and the visual design
is yours to own. Don't stop at "legible" — bring a real point of view:
choose a palette and typography with intent, give the hero genuine
presence, and let the page feel crafted. Tasteful gradients or textures,
cards, shadows, transitions, and micro-interactions are all welcome as
long as the copy stays the star and nothing gets in a reader's way. Show
what you can do.

The hero carries a centerpiece visual: an animation (or, at minimum, a
striking illustration) that shows what Lisa *does* without words — many
small pieces of work moving in parallel, each finishing in its own time,
until the whole board is done; a loop with steady momentum. Representative
motion, not an annotated diagram: no labels, captions, or text inside the
visual itself — if it needs words to make sense, it isn't done. Build it
self-contained: inline SVG animated with CSS, or pure CSS shapes — no
external assets, no video or GIF files.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A single static page (plain HTML/CSS, no build tooling required to view
  it) exists containing every section defined in T-003's outline, in that
  order, using the exact approved copy from T-004-T-007.
- The bottom-line message and CTA from T-004 are visible without scrolling
  on a standard desktop viewport.
- Visual hierarchy (headings, spacing, emphasis) makes the bottom-line-first
  structure legible at a glance — a visitor skimming should still get the
  T-002 bottom-line message even without reading every word.
- The page reads as deliberately designed, never browser-defaulted: a
  cohesive palette defined once as CSS custom properties, a type scale, a
  consistent spacing rhythm, and a hero treatment visually distinct from
  the body sections.
- Interactive elements (links, CTA, any toggles) have styled hover and
  focus states; the layout adapts at one or more intentional breakpoints
  rather than only shrinking.
- The hero contains a wordless centerpiece visual — an animation
  preferred, a representative illustration acceptable — conveying parallel
  work converging to done; no text, labels, or captions inside the visual.
- The hero visual is self-contained (inline SVG/CSS or pure CSS; no
  external assets, videos, or GIFs) and honors `prefers-reduced-motion`
  with a dignified static state.
- No placeholder or lorem-ipsum content remains; every section uses the
  copy produced by its owning ticket.
